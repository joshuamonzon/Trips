Istanbul

Dec 2 · 20h layover
  • Land 5:45am, fly out 1:45am. A whole day in a city they wanted to see, for free.
  • Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque at opening, the Grand Bazaar before the crowds, a long lunch.
  • Bosphorus at sunset, dinner in Karaköy, then the Turkish business lounge, which has a hammam.
  • They pass through again on the way home, both options.
No hotel nightTurkish's stopover program may give a free day room at exactly 20h. Check eligibility when booking. Turkey entry for US passports is visa-free or a ~$50 e-visa.

Hong Kong

Dec 3–6 · 3 nights
  • Land 5pm Dec 3. Soft landing: English everywhere, dim sum at 8am, the Peak at dusk, Star Ferry, Lock Cha tea house in Hong Kong Park.
  • Dec 4, Shenzhen tech day, 15 minutes by train: Huaqiangbei electronics market, the DJI flagship, a robotaxi ride, the drone show. This is why the China visa must be multiple-entry.
  • No guide. MTR and taxis.
Tuve, Tin Hau · ~$140 × 3Quiet design hotel, no tourist markup. Hotel ICON is the backup.

Zhangjiajie

Dec 6–9 · 3 nights
  • Dec 6: rail HK West Kowloon → Changsha 3h, lunch, Changsha → Zhangjiajie West 2.5h, driver an hour to Wulingyuan.
  • Dec 7: Tianmen Mountain. Longest cableway on earth, the glass skywalk, 999 steps to Heaven's Gate (there's an escalator).
  • Dec 8, her 50th: Avatar Mountain. Bailong Elevator, the tallest outdoor lift in the world, up to the Hallelujah pillar. Tianzi Mountain, Golden Whip Stream, then a private Tujia feast and cake.
  • Dec 9: the Glass Bridge at 8:30, then the 3h20 train to Chongqing. A guide both park days, the one place it earns its keep.

Days 11–18 · Dec 13–21 · Option A

North to the Wall.

Chengdu

Dec 13–16 · 3 nights
  • 1.5h train from Chongqing. The slowest city in China: bamboo chairs and jasmine tea in People's Park, the vegetarian lunch at Wenshu Monastery.
  • Pandas at the 8:30 feeding, no day trip, no rush. You sleep here.
  • Leshan Giant Buddha, the largest stone Buddha on earth, 233 ft, by boat at his feet.
  • Shufeng Yayun, the best Sichuan opera in the country. Mapo tofu at Chen Mapo, the original.
Buddhazen ~$120 or Niccolo ~$180 · budgeted at $160 × 3A courtyard inn by the monastery, or the tower.

Beijing

Dec 16–21 · 5 nights
  • Fly Chengdu → Beijing on Dec 16, 2.5h. The only flight inside China.
  • The Great Wall at Jinshanling, the wild half-restored stretch. Snow likely, almost no one there in December.
  • Forbidden City at 8:30 with a guide (the other day a guide earns it), Jingshan Hill at sunset, Peking opera at Liyuan Theatre.
  • Hutongs, Temple of Heaven tai chi at 8am, a hutong tea house, Peking duck at Siji Minfu, imperial-court farewell dinner at Bai Jia Da Yuan. 25–40°F: the real coat.

Days 11–18 · Dec 13–21 · Option B

South through the villages.

Zhaoxing

Dec 13–16 · 3 nights
  • Rail Chongqing → Guiyang 2h, Guiyang → Congjiang 1.5h, then the drive in. A 1,000-year-old Dong village in the Guizhou mountains: five drum towers, wind-and-rain bridges, rice terraces, wooden houses, no cars. Empty in December.
  • The Dong Grand Choir, UNESCO-listed polyphonic singing, performed in the village at night. That's the opera night, and it's real.
  • Day trips: Basha, the last Miao village where the men still carry muskets, and the Tang'an terraces. A local Dong guide for two days, cheap and essential.
  • Nobody they know has heard of it. That's the point.
Timber inn in the village · ~$100 × 3Best room in Zhaoxing. Wood, not marble.

Yangshuo

Dec 16–19 · 3 nights
  • Rail Congjiang → Guilin 1h, then an hour by car. The karst on the ¥20 note.
  • Bamboo raft down the Li River, cycling through paddies and villages, a tea plantation with a proper tasting.
  • Impression Liu Sanjie, Zhang Yimou's show staged on the actual river, 600 local fishermen, the mountains as the set.
  • 45–60°F, misty, quiet. A driver for two days.
Yangshuo Mountain Retreat · ~$130 × 3Riverside. Upgrade: Alila Yangshuo, a design hotel in a 1960s sugar mill, ~$250.
